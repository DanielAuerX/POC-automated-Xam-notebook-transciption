# Transcription conventions (prototype, v0)

This guide is used by the human transcriber and, later, as the prompt for a
vision model. It describes *diplomatic* transcription: write what is on the
page and leave normalisation to a later, reversible step.

## Page layout (Lloyd notebook BC_151_A2_1_009 and similar)

- One written page per spread. The facing page is blank.
- Two columns per ruled line: **English on the left**, **/Xam on the right**.
  The columns are roughly synchronised line by line, but they are not
  word-aligned translations.
- Lloyd's line numbers (5, 10, 15, 20) are in pencil in the left margin.
- Interlinear additions (small writing between ruled lines) become their own line
  with `"interlinear": true`.

## Glyph inventory: Lloyd hand → Unicode

| Glyph on page            | Write  | Meaning (status)                                          |
|--------------------------|--------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| circle with dot  ⊙       | `ʘ`    | bilabial click (confident)                                 |
| `!`                      | `ǃ`    | alveolar click (confident)                                 |
| `#` / `≠`                | `ǂ`    | palatal click (confident)                                  |
| capped stroke  ⊤         | `ǀ`    | **hypothesis**: dental click. Needs expert confirmation.   |
| cross  ×                 | `×`    | **unknown**. Kept as is until identified (lateral ǁ?).     |
| small x under a letter   | U+0353 `◌͓` | Lloyd's sub-letter x (e.g. `ǀk͓ŭḿ`), meaning to be confirmed |
| acute / macron / breve / tilde | combining marks ◌́ ◌̄ ◌̆ ◌̃ | stack in the order they are written, bottom to top |

## IPA layer

Transcriptions store Lloyd's spelling (`"xam"` field). The IPA version is
generated by `to_ipa.py`, so every phonological interpretation lives in one
mapping table that can be corrected and re-run over the whole corpus.

### Clicks (IPA click letters)

| IPA | Code point | Name                         | Lloyd glyph |
|-----|------------|------------------------------|-------------|
| ʘ   | U+0298     | bilabial click               | ⊙           |
| ǀ   | U+01C0     | dental click                 | ⊤ (hypothesis) |
| ǃ   | U+01C3     | (post)alveolar click         | !           |
| ǂ   | U+01C2     | palatoalveolar click         | # / ≠       |
| ǁ   | U+01C1     | alveolar lateral click       | not yet seen (× glyph?) |

Unicode calls U+01C3 "retroflex click" and U+01C2 "alveolar click". These
are legacy names; the characters are the IPA ones.

### Target notation

IPA as in Naumann (2016), Table 1. Click accompaniments are analysed as
consonant clusters (click + uvular/glottal consonant) following Güldemann
(2001) and Naumann (2016), Table 5. The *Orthography* column gives Naumann's
practical orthography (Tables 2, 5, 7), which follows Güldemann's (1998)
proposal for Southern African Khoisan.

Güldemann (2001) has no |Xam data. Its closest comparison is ǂKhomani
(!Ui, after Doke 1937), where Doke's `ǀkx` and `ǀkh` are analysed as
click + /x/ and click + /kh/ clusters. That supports reading Lloyd's `ǀk͓` as a
cluster, and shows that Lloyd's `ǀkh` is ambiguous.

- Naumann, Christfried. 2016. The phoneme inventory of Taa (West !Xoon
  dialect). In Rainer Vossen & Wilfrid H. G. Haacke (eds.), *Lone Tree:
  Scholarship in the Service of the Koon*, 311–351. Köln: Köppe.
- Güldemann, Tom. 2001. *Phonological regularities of consonant systems across
  Khoisan lineages*. University of Leipzig Papers on Africa, Languages and
  Literatures 16.

### Vowels in Naumann's inventory

Five vowels i e a o u. Naumann's orthography (Table 7) marks phonation after
the first vowel of a stem: breathy `ah`, pharyngealised `aq`, strident `aqh`,
glottalised `a'`, and nasalised `an` (second stem vowel only). Tone is high or
low per mora, written with acute and grave (`táà`). No Lloyd marks for breathy,
pharyngealised or glottalised vowels have been identified in our pages yet.

## Editorial markup

| Markup      | Meaning                                  |
|-------------|------------------------------------------|
| `_word_`    | underlined in the original               |
| `~~word~~`  | struck through                           |
| `^word^`    | inserted above the line                  |
| `[word?]`   | uncertain reading                        |
| `[?]`       | illegible / unidentified glyph           |
